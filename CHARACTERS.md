# Characters

## Dora

Dora is a young gigging bassist who works at the Blue Note between shows. She runs the till in the basement instrument shop. She has short, dark curls and small silver hoops. Her faded burgundy band tee has a cream screen print, and she wears charcoal jeans cuffed above scuffed black high-tops. A yellow guitar pick hangs from a cord around her neck, bright enough to catch your eye from across the desk.

Behind the till, Dora rolls up her sleeves and tilts her head slightly, as if she’s listening to someone try an instrument.
